Questions

Good to know
I've never played World of Warcraft. Is this for me?

Yes. Every step says exactly where to go and what to press, and game words explain themselves when you click them. Each Leveling Guide also has a How to Play tab with the basics.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, in this browser on this device. Close the page and come back any time. Another browser or device starts fresh.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Many players keep the guide open on their phone next to the game. Everything works on small screens.

The text is too small or hard to read.

Press the gear button at the bottom right. You can make the text bigger, turn on high contrast, or switch to an easy-read font.

Do I need addons?

No. The game works fine without them. A few make leveling easier, and each character's Addons page lists which ones are worth it.
