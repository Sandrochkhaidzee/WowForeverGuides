        Action Bars

        Where every ability should go, level by level. The main bar has what you press in every fight, on keys you reach without looking. Openers, stuns and emergency abilities go on Shift + number. Things you only use between fights go on the right bar, to click with the mouse. Hover over or tap a button to see what it does.

        What to change at this level

          How to set this up in game

          1. Press Esc → Interface → Action Bars. Turn on the Bottom Left Bar, the Bottom Right Bar and the Right Bar.
          2. Press Esc → Key Bindings. Find Bottom Left Action Button 1 to 12 and set them to Shift + 1 … Shift + =. Then set Bottom Right Action Button 1 and 2 to Alt + 1 and Alt + 2.
          3. Open your spellbook (P) and drag each ability to its place. Poisons, sharpening stones, bandages and food are items: drag them from your bags (B) instead.
          4. Turn on Lock Action Bars in the same Action Bars menu so you can't drag spells off by accident.
          How to Play

          Quests

          • A yellow ! over someone's head means they have a quest for you. Click them and press Accept.
          • A yellow ? means you can hand in a finished quest there.
          • Press L to see your quests. Each one says what to do and where.
          • Most of your XP comes from quests, so do every one you find.

          Fighting

          • Click an enemy to target it, walk right up to it, and press your attack key. Rogues fight up close.
          • Fight one enemy at a time. Rogues hit hard but only wear leather. Sneak past the enemies you don't need with Stealth.
          • Enemy level colours: grey gives no XP, green is easy, yellow is a fair fight, orange is hard and red means run.
          • A golden dragon border on the portrait means elite. Don't fight those alone.

          After every fight

          • Health low? Eat (Alt + 2). You sit down by yourself while eating. Don't move until the bar is full.
          • From level 10, use a bandage first (Alt + 1), then eat. You make bandages yourself with First Aid from the cloth enemies drop. Buy food from food vendors.
          • Rogues use energy, not mana, so you never need to drink.

          Getting stronger

          • Every 2 levels, visit a rogue trainer and learn everything you can afford.
          • Every level from 10, press N and spend a talent point. See the Talents tab.
          • New abilities go into your spellbook (P). The Action Bars tab shows where to put them.

          Getting around

          • Talk to the innkeeper in each new town and choose "Make this inn your home". Your Hearthstone then brings you back there.
          • Talk to every flight master (the person with a hippogryph or gryphon) you pass, so you can fly there later.
          • If you die, you appear as a ghost at a graveyard. Run back to your body and click "Resurrect". As a night elf you're a fast wisp while dead (Wisp Spirit).
          • Log out inside an inn to get bonus XP next time.

          Addons

          Addons are free extras that improve the game's interface. You don't need any, but a few make leveling much easier. The Addons page lists which ones to get (must-have, recommended, optional), which to skip, and how to install them.

          Waypoint arrow: with the TomTom addon, click a pin on any Quest Guide map and press Copy /way. In game, press Enter, paste with Ctrl + V and press Enter again. An arrow points the way. /way reset all clears it.

          The one combo to learn

          1. Stealth and walk up to the enemy. Steal from humanoids with Pick Pocket first.
          2. Open with Cheap Shot (from level 26) or Sinister Strike.
          3. Slice and Dice as soon as you have 1 or 2 combo points.
          4. Sinister Strike until you have 5 combo points.
          5. Eviscerate. Keep Slice and Dice running, and Gouge or Kick the enemy when it starts casting a spell.
          Talents

          From level 10 you get one talent point each level. Press N, pick the Combat tab, and click the talent from this list. If one is greyed out, take the next one and come back.
